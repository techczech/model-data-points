Data points

A data point is one small experiment on an AI model: what was tested, on which models, why, and what happened. Entries tagged Benchmark are the longer comparisons from the Benchmarks section, listed here so there is one place to browse.

28 Oct 2026OCRData point

OCR comparison: Unlimited-OCR 2, Docling and MinerU on academic PDFs

Unlimited-OCR 2 · openDocling 2.93 · openMinerU 3.1.9 · open

On a DGX Spark, Docling converted 10 papers in 3.5 min, Unlimited-OCR 2 in 31 min and MinerU in 62 min, with no accuracy gain for the slower tools.
